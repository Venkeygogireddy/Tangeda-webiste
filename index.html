About Tangeda

Tangeda is a historic village with deep cultural roots and long-standing traditions. It has grown over the years while preserving its heritage, respect for elders, and close-knit community spirit.

Rich Heritage

Founded centuries ago, Tangeda carries the legacy of The King Anumula Machireddy Garu. The village is home to historic temples, age-old customs, and stories passed down through generations.

Cultural Harmony

People of different communities live together with unity and mutual respect. Festivals, events, and social gatherings bring everyone together throughout the year.

Festivals of Tangeda

Festivals in Tangeda are celebrated with devotion, color, and togetherness.

Sankranthi

Harvest festival with rangoli, traditional games, and family gatherings.

Ganesh Chaturthi

Idols of Lord Ganesha are installed across the village and immersed with grand processions.

Muharram & Other Events

Marking unity beyond religion, people come together to participate in multiple cultural and spiritual events.
